Laxmi Prasad Devkota (1909–1959) was a legendary Nepalese poet, novelist, and playwright, widely honored as "Mahakavi" (The Great Poet). Known for his profound empathy, rapid writing speed, and revolutionary impact on the Nepali language, he is celebrated as one of the founding fathers of modern Nepali literature.Quick FactsBirth: November 12, 1909 (in Kathmandu, Nepal)Death: September 14, 1959 (in Kathmandu, Nepal)Key Titles: Mahakavi, Poet with the Golden HeartNotable Works: Muna Madan, Shakuntala, Sulochana, Pagal
